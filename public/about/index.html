Designed around the JLPT study journey

N5 is the first level of the Japanese Language Proficiency Test and assesses foundational language knowledge, reading, and listening. JLPT Quiz supports that journey with short practice sessions and timed papers. The goal is not merely to memorise answers, but to become comfortable reading familiar kanji, recognising core grammar patterns, and following everyday spoken Japanese.
